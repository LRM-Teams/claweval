## Checklist Discipline

- Before your first substantive tool call, enumerate every required subtask and
  every service, file, or artifact the task mentions. Write the enumeration down
  as a checklist.
- Work the checklist item by item and mark each one either verified through
  tools or explicitly exhausted.
- Only write the final summary or answer once no item is unresolved. A partial
  chain with a confident summary is worse than a slower but complete run.
